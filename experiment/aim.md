## Aim of experiment
Extraction of bipolar SPICE Gummel-Poon parameters related to B-C junction Capacitance-Voltage (C-V) characteristics

### The following SPICE parameters are extracted:
- CJC, B-C zero-bias depletion capacitance
- VJC, B-C built-in potential
- MC, B-C junction exponential factor
- FC, Coefficient for forward-bias depletion capacitance
