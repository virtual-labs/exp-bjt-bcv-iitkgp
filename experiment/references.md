## References
#### Books
1) Ian Getreu, Modeling the Bipolar Transistor, Tektronix, Inc., 1976. (This is the definitive guide. It provides the exact, practical methods for measuring the B-C capacitance and extracting CJC, VJC, and MJC from the C-V plots.)

2) S. M. Sze and Kwok K. Ng, Physics of Semiconductor Devices, 3rd ed., Wiley, 2006.
(Provides the core semiconductor physics explaining the B-C depletion capacitance and how the $1/C^2$ vs. $V$ plot relates to the junction's doping profile and built-in potential.)

3) G. Massobrio and P. Antognetti, Semiconductor Device Modeling with SPICE, 2nd ed., McGraw-Hill, 1993.
(Details the specific Gummel-Poon SPICE equations for the B-C capacitance ($C_{bc}$) and explains the roles of CJC, VJC, MJC, and the reverse transit time TR.)
